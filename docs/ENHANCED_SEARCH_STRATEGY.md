# Enhanced GitHub Search Strategy - Complete Vision Based

## Overview
This enhanced strategy is based on the **complete 100% built-out vision** of the Bitcoin Estate Planning Platform, organized by **likelihood of finding existing solutions** (High → Medium → Low).

**Key Insight**: Focus more search effort on areas with **HIGH likelihood** of finding complete solutions, while being prepared to build custom solutions for **LOW likelihood** areas.

---

## 🎯 Likelihood Classification

### HIGH Likelihood (80-100% chance of finding solutions)
**Characteristics:**
- Common, well-established patterns
- Standard implementations
- Widely-used libraries/frameworks
- Generic functionality (not Bitcoin-specific)
- Well-documented solutions

**Search Strategy**: **Aggressive** - Multiple queries, deep dives, expect to find complete solutions

### MEDIUM Likelihood (40-79% chance of finding solutions)
**Characteristics:**
- Somewhat specialized
- Bitcoin/crypto-related but common
- Combination of standard patterns
- May need adaptation

**Search Strategy**: **Moderate** - Targeted searches, expect partial solutions or patterns

### LOW Likelihood (0-39% chance of finding solutions)
**Characteristics:**
- Highly specialized
- Unique to estate planning
- Bitcoin-specific inheritance
- Novel combinations
- Custom business logic

**Search Strategy**: **Minimal** - Basic searches for inspiration, expect to build custom
